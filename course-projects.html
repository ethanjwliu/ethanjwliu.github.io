How readable are forward-looking statements?

I used sentence length and the share of complex words to calculate the Gunning Fog index. I scored each filing both as a whole and for sentences containing terms such as “will” or “might.” The saved table has 100 rows and 21 columns.

Measuring tone with financial dictionaries

I counted positive, negative, and litigious words in 100 filing files. Dividing each count by the document’s word count made it possible to compare filings of different lengths. The notebook keeps both the raw counts and the rates.
